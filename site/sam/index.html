Sam

Your AI voice mentor for Customer Success

Sam is a conversational agent built with ElevenLabs Conversational AI. It explains the key concepts of Customer Success, gives practical advice to junior CSMs and helps candidates prepare for interviews for any Customer Success position, from CSM or Onboarding Specialist to Customer Success Lead or Head of CS. It answers using its own knowledge base and speaks English and Spanish. Press the button and ask, for example: “What is churn?” or “Can you run a mock interview for a Customer Success Manager role?”
